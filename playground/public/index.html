How it works

  

    
01_RAW
Your app has raw state
hp: 38, maxHp: 250, epoch timestamps, cents, IDs, positions. Jev reads numbers as text, can't count, and gets distracted by irrelevant fields.

    
02_PROJECT
jev-state does the math in code
band(), count(), time(), and prune() turn it into "critical", "3 or more times", "a few minutes ago".

    
03_DECIDE
Jev makes the decision
Same model, same question, same rules. On a 420-call benchmark, accuracy went from 74% to 100% with 43% fewer tokens.
